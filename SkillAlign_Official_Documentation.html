Executive Overview

SkillAlign is a complete recruitment platform that ranks candidates against jobs using a transparent Fit Score, then manages every step of the hiring journey — shortlisting, interviews, offers, and final outcomes — within one connected system.

🎯

Explainable Matching

Every score breaks down into skills, experience, education, and work mode. No black boxes.

⚙️

Controlled Pipeline

A 21-stage lifecycle enforced by the workflow engine. No step can be skipped.

🔐

Role-Separated Access

JWT-based RBAC ensures each role sees and does only what it is permitted to.

📄

End-to-End Offers

Draft → HR approval → PDF generation → candidate response. Fully tracked.

📊

Complete Audit Trail

Every state transition, actor, and timestamp is permanently logged in the audit_logs table.

📨

Automated Communication

Interview invitations and offer letters dispatched via SendGrid with secure token links.

The Problem SkillAlign Solves

Traditional recruitment is slowed down by manual friction, surface-level keyword matching, and fragmented tooling. SkillAlign solves these six fundamental pain points:

📥

Resume Overload

High-volume applicants lead to screening fatigue, slow response times, and missed top talent.

🔍

Shallow Keyword Matching

Mentioning a tool is not mastery. Traditional systems ignore depth, recency, and contextual evidence.

❓

Unexplainable Decisions

Opaque scoring and arbitrary rejections create compliance risks and candidate frustration.

🔀

Fragmented Tooling

Sourcing, scheduling, feedback, and offers scattered across separate systems with no single source of truth.

📭

Poor Candidate Experience

Radio silence and awkward scheduling emails damage employer brand reputation.

🧩

Missing Auditability

Decisions lost in private notes leave organizations vulnerable to disputes and compliance issues.

SkillAlign's Solution: A unified, evidence-based matching engine paired with a 21-stage state-machine workflow that enforces accountability, transparency, and seamless collaboration from resume upload to signed offer.

07

Authentication

All protected endpoints require a valid JWT Bearer token. Role claims inside the token are verified on every request by the dependency-injection layer in FastAPI.

✉️

Email & Password

Standard login — password verified against bcrypt hash. Available for all roles.

📱

Phone OTP

Candidate login via Twilio SMS. OTP is valid for 5 minutes, rate-limited by SlowAPI.

🔑

Password Reset

Cryptographically random token, SHA-256 hashed at rest, delivered via SendGrid. One-use and time-limited.

🔒

Session Tokens

HS256-signed JWT with expiry and role claims. Signing out immediately invalidates the session.

Candidate self-registration: Candidates register via a public POST /api/auth/register endpoint. HR and Recruiter accounts are provisioned by the Administrator only.

Candidate Journey

1

Register & Build Profile

Create account, enter location, total experience, education qualification, and work mode preference.

2

Upload Resume

Upload PDF, DOCX, or TXT. A Celery task processes the file asynchronously — no manual entry needed.

3

Review Extracted Skills

See which skills were detected from the resume, their proficiency signals, and the supporting evidence text snippets.

4

Get Matched to Jobs

Once the profile is ready, Fit Scores are automatically calculated against all active jobs. Track application status from the candidate dashboard.

5

Receive Interview Invitation

A SendGrid email arrives with proposed time slots and a secure, one-use selection link — no login required.

6

Select a Slot & Attend

Click the link, choose a slot. System cancels the remaining slots automatically. Confirmation email follows with the meeting link.

7

Receive the Offer

If the interview outcome is GO, a ReportLab-generated PDF offer arrives via a S3 presigned link in email.

8

Accept or Decline

One click on the secure token link. Response is recorded instantly, all parties notified, and the match_result row updated.

Job Creation & Requirements

Jobs are created by HR via POST /api/jobs (HR / Admin only). When a job is set to Active, the backend automatically enqueues a Celery matching task against all available candidates.

Role Details

Title, department, client name, and full description.

Required Skills

Up to 20 skills — each with a weight (1–5) and type: MUST-HAVE or NICE-TO-HAVE.

Experience Threshold

Minimum years of relevant experience. Factors into the 20-pt experience component.

Work Arrangement

Remote / Hybrid / On-site. Mismatch reduces the work-mode component from 10 to 4 pts.

PostgreSQL3 / 5MUST-HAVEImportant — meaningful contribution to the score.

11

Skill Taxonomy

A curated master list — maintained by the Administrator — ensures that "React", "React.js", and "ReactJS" all resolve to the same canonical skill entry. Every skill used in job requirements and candidate profiles references this taxonomy, making matching consistent across all records in the skills table.

📄

Resume-Detected Skills

Found automatically during parsing. Backed by evidence snippets stored in candidate_skills.evidence_text.

✍️

Manually Declared Skills

Added by the candidate with a self-assessed proficiency level (Beginner / Intermediate / Expert). Stored with source = MANUAL.

Both skill sources are scored during matching, but resume-backed skills with evidence receive a higher proficiency factor than unverified manual entries.

Matching Engine & Fit Score

The matching engine is the core intelligence of SkillAlign. It evaluates candidate profiles against job requirements using a deterministic, rule-based algorithm (0–100 scale), persisted to match_results.

Design Principle: The Fit Score is computed by a transparent, deterministic mathematical formula — not an unpredictable black-box model. Given identical inputs, it always yields the identical authoritative score.
Figure 3 · Matching Engine Flow

Figure 3. The deterministic matching engine compares job and candidate data and persists the result to the match_results table — the central record linking every job-candidate pair.

Score Model Breakdown

Skills & Proficiency  60%
Experience  20%
Education  10%
Work Mode  10%
Skills & Proficiency (max 60 pts) Experience Match (max 20 pts) Education Level (max 10 pts) Work Mode Compatibility (max 10 pts)
ComponentMaxScoring Logic
Skills + Proficiency60 ptsWeighted sum: (Σ factor × weight) / Σ weights × 60. Factor depends on source (resume/manual) and proficiency level. Evidence boosts the factor.
Experience20 pts20 if fully met · 12 if ≥70% · 6 if partial · 0 if none
Education10 pts10 if a degree is present in the profile · 0 otherwise
Work Mode10 pts10 if compatible (remote/hybrid/exact match) · 4 for mismatch
Worked Example — Rahul vs. Backend Developer:
Python (Expert + evidence): 5.00 pts · FastAPI (Intermediate + evidence): 3.24 pts · PostgreSQL (Resume detected): 2.55 pts · Docker (Missing): 0
Skill Score = (10.79 / 14) × 60 = 46.2 · Experience: 20 · Education: 10 · Work Mode: 10
Overall Fit Score = 86.2 / 100

An optional AI narrative (via Gemini) can supplement the score with a plain-language strengths/gaps summary and suggested interview questions. It is clearly labelled and does not modify the authoritative Fit Score.

Approve for interview (pipeline advances) or reject with a documented reason (terminal state).

Figure 6. Every offer state is persisted to the offers table. Any post-approval modification by the recruiter auto-revokes HR approval and re-queues the offer for review.

Compensation

Base salary, fixed/variable pay, performance bonus, joining bonus.

Benefits & Equity

Other benefits, stock options, or RSU details where applicable.

Joining Details

Start date, work location, work arrangement (remote/hybrid/on-site).

Offer Expiry

Candidate must respond before this date. Offers expire automatically via a scheduled job.

Salary Band Enforcement: Salary outside the configured min/max band requires a written justification before the offer can be submitted for HR review. Enforced at the API layer.

Candidate Response & Outcomes

When an offer is sent, the candidate receives a SendGrid email with an S3 presigned link to the PDF and a secure response token. No platform login is required.

✅

On Acceptance

Offer → ACCEPTED · pipeline_state → HIRED · all parties notified · audit record created.

❌

On Decline

Offer → REJECTED · pipeline → BLACKLISTED · 180-day cooling-off row written to candidate_blacklists · parties notified.

180-Day Cooling-Off: A declined formal offer writes a record to candidate_blacklists with expires_at = now() + 180 days. The candidate is suppressed from new match results and slot proposals until the record expires automatically.

Audit Trail

Every significant pipeline transition writes an immutable row to the audit_logs table capturing: actor_id, action, entity_type, entity_id, old_state, new_state, and created_at. Records cannot be deleted or modified.

CategoryEvents Logged
OFFERDraft created, submitted, approved, rejected, sent, accepted, declined
INTERVIEWRequested, slots sent, slot selected, confirmed, completed, feedback submitted
HM_REVIEWShortlist received, candidate approved, candidate rejected
SOURCINGCandidate claimed, screened, shortlisted, submitted to HM
TASKSTask created, assigned, status changed, completed

Administrators access the full log via GET /api/admin/hiring-audit-logs with filters for category, action, date range, candidate, job, and actor.

PasswordsbcryptOne-way adaptive hash — even if the DB is compromised, passwords are unreadable.
